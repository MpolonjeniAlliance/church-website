🌍 VISION 2037: Building a Legacy of Faith
1,500‑seat auditorium, 5,000 souls, 4 strategic pillars, E10 million goal

VISION 2037

Mpolonjeni Alliance Church is constructing a new state-of-the-art auditorium to accommodate its rapidly growing congregation and to serve as a center for community outreach and Christian-based events. Located in a fast-developing area, the church has experienced steady growth, with its current building no longer meeting the needs of its expanding membership. Vision 2037 is a long-term project to complete a modern auditorium designed to host 1,500 worshipers.

We are extending our current church structure to accommodate more families, expand our ministries, and create a greater impact in our community. Vision 2037 also seeks to provide a home for international brethren who can join us through our live streaming services. This vision is not only aimed at touching local souls but even from all corners of the world. We believe God is calling us to enlarge our tent and build a legacy of faith for generations to come.

⛪ Church History: Our Heritage – The Story of MPAC
Founded 2000, tent crusades, Rev. S.P. Dlamini, first elders

Foundation and Apostolic Vision

Mpolonjeni Alliance Church was birthed from a strategic apostolic vision in the year 2000. At the time, Rev. S.P. Dlamini, Pastor of Mbabane Alliance Church, was driven by a divine mandate to expand the reach of the Gospel beyond the city limits. This vision manifested through a series of intensive tent crusades conducted across the Sidwashini, Manzana, and Mpolonjeni regions.

The Season of Harvest

Between 1998 and 2000, three pivotal tent crusades were held within the Mpolonjeni community. These gatherings became a catalyst for spiritual awakening; countless souls were won, and many made the transformative decision to receive Jesus Christ as their Lord and Savior.

Establishing a Local Sanctuary

As the number of converts grew, it became clear that the new believers needed a local spiritual home. To spare the brethren from the long journey to Mbabane, Rev. Dlamini moved to establish a permanent presence in Mpolonjeni. Through dedicated negotiation and engagement with traditional structures, land was successfully secured for the ministry.

Commissioning the Leaders

With the land secured, Mpolonjeni Alliance Church (MPAC) was formally established. Pastor Dlamini commissioned Mr. B. Tsela and Mr. T. Mthupha as the first elders, charging them with the stewardship of this new frontier.

MPAC Today

Today, MPAC stands as a vibrant beacon of hope. We continue to honor our roots by relentlessly pursuing the vision of reaching souls and transforming lives through the Gospel of Jesus Christ. We give God all the glory for His unwavering faithfulness over the past two decades.
