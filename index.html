Our Story & Apostolic Foundation

Mpolonjeni Alliance Church is situated in the Mpolonjeni Community within Mbabane, Eswatini, in the Hhohho Region. Founded in the year 2000 through the apostolic vision of Rev. S.P. Dlamini, pastor of Mbabane Alliance Church, the church emerged from powerful tent crusades across Sidwashini, Manzana, and Mpolonjeni. These crusades, driven by a divine mandate to extend the Gospel beyond city limits, birthed a thriving congregation of believers hungry for God’s presence.

Rev. Dlamini commissioned the first elders, Mr. B. Tsela and Mr. T. Mthupha, entrusting them with leadership. From those early gatherings under canvas, MPAC grew into a beacon of hope, embracing the Alliance family’s core values — evangelism, discipleship, compassionate ministry, and prayer. Today, our church stands as a vibrant spiritual hub where thousands have encountered Christ, and we continue to raise disciples who impact every sector of society.

We are unapologetically committed to transformation — spiritually, emotionally, and socially. Guided by the Great Commission, we worship with passion, serve with humility, and extend love to the vulnerable in Mpolonjeni and beyond.

The harvest is plentiful, and we have been called to be laborers. MPAC exists to bring people back to God and to teach them to walk as His disciples. We are reaching out to every soul, leaving no one behind. EVERY SOUL MATTERS IN THE KINGDOM
— Rev. M. Dlamini (Senior Pastor)

VISION 2037: Building a Legacy

VISION 2037

Mpolonjeni Alliance Church is extending its current building to accommodate its rapidly growing congregation. Located in a fast-developing area, the church has experienced steady growth, and its existing facility no longer meets the needs of its expanding membership. Vision 2037 is a long-term project to complete a modern extension designed to host 1,500+ worshippers.

🏛️ Current Sanctuary
🌱 Future Auditorium (Concept)

We are extending our current church structure to accommodate more families, expand our ministries, and create a greater impact in our community. Vision 2037 also seeks to provide a home for international brethren who can join us through our live streaming services. This vision is not only aimed at touching local souls but even from all corners of the world. We believe God is calling us to enlarge our tent and build a legacy of faith for generations to come.

⛪ Church History

Foundation and Apostolic Vision (2000)

Mpolonjeni Alliance Church was birthed from a strategic apostolic vision. Rev. S.P. Dlamini, Pastor of Mbabane Alliance Church, was driven by a divine mandate to expand the Gospel beyond the city limits through tent crusades across Sidwashini, Manzana, and Mpolonjeni.

The Season of Harvest (1998–2000)

Three pivotal tent crusades brought spiritual awakening; countless souls were won, and many received Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior.

Establishing a Local Sanctuary

As converts grew, land was secured through engagement with traditional structures. Mpolonjeni Alliance Church (MPAC) was formally established.

Commissioning the Leaders

Pastor Dlamini commissioned Mr. B. Tsela and Mr. T. Mthupha as the first elders, charging them with stewardship of this new frontier.

MPAC Today

Today, MPAC stands as a vibrant beacon of hope, relentlessly pursuing the vision of reaching souls and transforming lives through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
