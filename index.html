About Us

Mpolonjeni Alliance Church is situated in the Mpolonjeni Community in Mbabane, Eswatini, in the Hhohho Region. The church is in close proximity to Nhlanganisweni High School, making it a spiritual landmark for both the local community and surrounding areas. Founded in the year 2000 through the apostolic vision of Rev. S.P. Dlamini, our church has grown from a small tent‑crusade movement into a vibrant, Spirit‑filled congregation dedicated to transforming lives through the grace and power of God.

Our mission is to bring people back to God and to teach them to become Christ's disciples. We believe in holistic transformation — spiritually, emotionally, socially, and physically. Through our various ministries (Children, Youth, Women, Men, Worship, Prayer, and Outreach), we serve as a beacon of hope, providing practical care, feeding programs, counselling, and community development initiatives.

We are strategically located near Nhlanganisweni High School, which allows us to engage with young people and families, offering mentorship, discipleship, and spiritual guidance. Our doors are open to everyone, regardless of background, as we build a family of faith, love, and unity. Under the leadership of our pastoral team and elders, we continue to pursue the Great Commission, reaching souls, making disciples, and preparing for the fulfillment of Vision 2037 — a 1500‑seat auditorium that will serve as a hub for worship, community outreach, and kingdom advancement.

Core Values: Worship, Discipleship, Prayer, Unity, Service, and Generosity. We invite you to join us in this life‑changing journey.

VISION 2037: Building a Legacy

VISION 2037

Mpolonjeni Alliance Church is constructing a new state-of-the-art auditorium to accommodate its rapidly growing congregation and to serve as a center for community outreach and Christian-based events. Located in a fast-developing area, the church has experienced steady growth, with its current building no longer meeting the needs of its expanding membership. Vision 2037 is a long-term project to complete a modern auditorium designed to host 1,500 worshipers.

⛪ Complete Church History

Foundation and Apostolic Vision (2000)

Mpolonjeni Alliance Church was birthed from a strategic apostolic vision. Rev. S.P. Dlamini, Pastor of Mbabane Alliance Church, was driven by a divine mandate to expand the Gospel beyond the city limits through tent crusades across Sidwashini, Manzana, and Mpolonjeni.

The Season of Harvest (1998–2000)

Three pivotal tent crusades brought spiritual awakening; countless souls were won, and many received Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior.

Establishing a Local Sanctuary

As converts grew, land was secured through engagement with traditional structures. Mpolonjeni Alliance Church (MPAC) was formally established.

Commissioning the Leaders

Pastor Dlamini commissioned Mr. B. Tsela and Mr. T. Mthupha as the first elders, charging them with stewardship of this new frontier.

MPAC Today

Today, MPAC stands as a vibrant beacon of hope, relentlessly pursuing the vision of reaching souls and transforming lives through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
